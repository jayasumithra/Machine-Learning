Standardized Deep Learning Preprocessing Flow
Chest radiographs from diverse clinical scanners exhibit significant variations in dimensions, bit-depths, dynamic range, and tonal contrast. Our preprocessing module transforms heterogeneous raw input radiographs into a mathematically consistent tensor:
Raw Radiograph → RGB Conversion → Bicubic / Area Resizing (224×224×3) → CLAHE Contrast Equalization → Float32 Normalization [0.0, 1.0] → Model Tensor Input
