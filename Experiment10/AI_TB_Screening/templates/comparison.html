1. CNN Baseline

CUSTOM 4-BLOCK CONVNET

A simple sequential convolutional network built from scratch. Ideal as an experimental baseline to prove that transfer learning yields superior feature generalization over randomly initialized weights on small datasets.

2. EfficientNetB0

COMPOUND SCALED BACKBONE (RECOMMENDED)

Balances depth, width, and resolution using inverted bottleneck residual blocks (MBConv) and Squeeze-and-Excitation attention. Delivers top-tier accuracy while maintaining small model weight size (~16MB) and fast CPU inference.

3. ResNet50

DEEP RESIDUAL NETWORK

Uses identity skip connections to solve the vanishing gradient problem across 50 layers. Extremely robust on large medical corpuses, with higher parameter capacity (~25M parameters) and slightly higher compute requirements.
