Clinical Screening Protocol & Instructions
  1. Select a standard Posteroanterior (PA) or Anteroposterior (AP) chest radiograph.
  2. Ensure the image shows bilateral lung apices down to the costophrenic angles.
  3. The system preprocesses the input image by converting to 3-channel RGB, resizing to 224×224 pixels, and normalizing to [0, 1].
  4. Inference produces an experimental screening probability score.
  5. Explainable AI (Grad-CAM) highlights the convolutional feature map regions that drove the model's prediction.
